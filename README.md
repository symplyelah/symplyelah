# Hello! It's nice to meet you 👋

I am a data scientist, trainer, digital marketer, and web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I work on data science education, analytics, visualization, and technology projects, with a strong interest in helping beginners and professionals build practical data skills using R and Python. I am also the co-founder of Zelah Ltd, an export and logistics company, and Solelytics, a data and analytics initiative focused on using data-driven insights to support smarter decision-making and business growth.

I love creating beautiful visualizations, working with R and Python, exploring AI applications, and building educational content for aspiring data professionals.

Outside of tech and data, I enjoy _books, movies, lifelong learning,_ and _supporting Arsenal FC_. I am also a fan of *The Lord of the Rings* trilogy and enjoy community building and mentoring women in technology.

I live in Lagos, Nigeria with my husband and two kids.
